Adjust Audio Volume

Overview

Adjust Audio Volume makes an audio file, or the sound of a video, louder or quieter, or normalizes its loudness to a consistent level, entirely in your browser. The file is never uploaded.

How to change audio volume

Follow these steps on your device.

  • Import one MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus, or other audio file, or a video with sound.
  • Drag the slider, type an exact percentage from 0% to 400%, or pick a preset. Or check Auto to normalize the loudness instead.
  • Play the file to hear the new level.
  • Export and save the adjusted file.

Normalize loudness

Normalize loudness first measures how loud the whole file sounds (its integrated loudness, following the EBU R128 method), then raises or lowers it by one fixed amount to reach −16 LUFS, a common level for podcasts and streaming. The loudest peaks are kept at or below −1 dBTP, so normalizing does not clip: audio with sharp peaks, such as a single loud clap, may end up a little quieter than the target. Mono audio is measured as if played on two speakers, so it ends up as loud as stereo. The file is processed twice, once to measure and once to adjust, and the progress bar covers both. Silent audio, and clips shorter than 0.4 seconds, cannot be measured. The preview plays the original level while this option is selected.

Format and quality

The audio is re-encoded in its original format: MP3 stays MP3, AAC/M4A stays M4A, and so on, at a similar bitrate; WAV keeps its bit depth and FLAC stays lossless. Formats this browser build cannot write, and 7.1 E-AC-3 (which it can write only up to 5.1), are converted to AAC in M4A. Tags such as title and artist are kept, and so is JPEG or PNG cover art in MP3, M4A, and FLAC files. From a video, only the audio is saved, as a standalone audio file that matches its audio, as in Trim Audio. Raising the volume above 100% can clip the loudest parts; Auto (normalize loudness) raises the level without clipping. 0% keeps a silent audio track.

Local processing

Measuring and encoding run on your device, so the file is not uploaded. The session is not restored after you leave or reload the page.

FAQ

  • Why is Export disabled? The volume is set to 100%, which would not change anything. Pick another level or check Auto.
  • Does the preview include the change? Yes for a set level, including levels above 100% where the browser supports it. With Auto, the loudness is measured during export, so the preview plays the original.
  • Can I choose the loudness target? No. Normalizing always aims at −16 LUFS with a −1 dBTP peak ceiling.
  • Can I use a video? Yes. Its first audio track is adjusted and saved as an audio file; the picture is not included. Use Adjust Video Volume to keep the video.
