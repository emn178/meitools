Convert Audio

Overview

Convert Audio changes an audio file, or the sound of a video, to MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, or Opus entirely in your browser. The file is never uploaded.

How to convert audio

Import one audio file or a video with sound, wait for it to be analyzed, choose an output format, then export the converted audio file to your device. MP3 is selected by default.

Formats and quality

When the source audio already uses the codec of the chosen format, it is copied into the new file without re-encoding, so quality is unchanged. Otherwise the audio is re-encoded: MP3 and M4A (AAC) at 192 kb/s, OGG (Vorbis) at high quality, Opus at 160 kb/s, and lossless FLAC or 16-bit WAV. Sample rate and channels stay automatic. Converting a lossy file to FLAC or WAV does not restore detail that was already lost.

Local processing

Analysis and conversion happen on your device. Cover art is not carried over, while common tags such as title and artist are kept when the output format supports them. The session is not restored after you leave or reload the page.

FAQ

  • Why is Export disabled? The file is already in the chosen format, so there is nothing to convert. Pick another format.
  • Can I set the bitrate or sample rate? No. Bitrate, sample rate, and channels are chosen automatically.
  • Can I use a video? Yes. Its first audio track is converted and saved as an audio file; the picture is not included. Use Convert Video to change a video’s format instead.
  • Can I convert several files at once? No. Convert one file at a time.
