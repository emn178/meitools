Merge Audio

Overview

Merge Audio joins two or more audio files, or the sound of videos, into one audio file in the order you choose, entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

How to merge audio

Follow these steps on your device.

  • Import two or more MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus, or other audio files, or videos with sound. You can add more files later.
  • Drag the files, or use the arrow buttons on a phone, to put them in order. Select a file to listen to it, or remove it with ×.
  • Choose an output format. MP3 is selected by default.
  • Export and save the merged file.

Formats and quality

The files are joined back to back with no gap and re-encoded once into the chosen format: MP3 and M4A (AAC) at 192 kb/s, OGG (Vorbis) at high quality, Opus at 160 kb/s, or lossless FLAC or WAV. Files with different sample rates or channel counts are matched automatically: the highest sample rate the format supports is used, and mono files join stereo ones as stereo. MP3 holds at most stereo, and surround sound becomes 5.1 or 7.1 in the other formats.

Local processing

Analysis and merging happen on your device. From a video only the sound is used; the picture, cover art, and tags such as title and artist are not carried over. The session is not restored after you leave or reload the page.

FAQ

  • Why is Export disabled? Add at least two files, wait for them to be analyzed, and remove any file marked “No audio track”.
  • Is there a gap or a crossfade between files? No. Each file starts right where the previous one ends.
  • Can I merge only part of a file? Not here. Trim it first with Trim Audio, then merge the result.
  • Is there a limit on files? No fixed limit, but very long or many files take more time and memory, especially on phones.
