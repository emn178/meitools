Remove Audio Section

Overview

Remove Audio Section cuts one unwanted part out of an audio file, or out of the sound of a video, and joins what is left, entirely in your browser. The result keeps the original audio format, and your file is never uploaded.

How to remove a section

Follow these steps on your device.

  • Import one MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus, or other audio file, or a video with sound.
  • Drag the start and end handles, or type exact times, to select the part to remove.
  • Preview the remaining audio to hear how the two sides join.
  • Export and save the file.

Format and quality

Removing a section from the middle joins the audio before and after it. To keep that join free of clicks, the audio is cut to the exact sample and encoded once: WAV, FLAC, and ALAC stay lossless, and MP3, AAC/M4A, Vorbis, Opus, and similar formats are re-encoded in the same format at a similar bitrate. Removing a section at the very start or end is simply a trim, handled exactly like Trim Audio, so MP3, AAC/M4A, and Vorbis are copied without re-encoding. Tags such as title and artist are kept, and so is JPEG or PNG cover art in MP3, M4A, and FLAC files. From a video, only the audio is saved, as a standalone audio file that matches its audio, as in Trim Audio.

Local processing

Everything runs on your device, so the file is not uploaded. The session is not restored after you leave or reload the page.

FAQ

  • Can I remove several sections? Remove one section, export, and open the result to remove the next.
  • Why is Export disabled? Select the section to remove; at least 0.1 seconds of audio must remain.
  • Can I change the format or bitrate? No. The source format is kept and encoding is chosen automatically.
  • Can I remove a section of a video? This tool saves only the audio. Use Remove Video Section to keep the picture.
