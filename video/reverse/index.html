Reverse Video

Overview

Reverse Video makes a complete video play backward entirely in your browser. The picture is re-encoded in reverse order, and the audio is either reversed along with it or removed. Nothing is uploaded.

How to reverse a video

Import a video, choose whether to reverse or remove its audio, and export it. The preview plays the original video; the exported file plays backward.

Long and high-resolution videos

Reversing needs every frame of a stretch of video at once, so the video is processed in short sections that fit in memory and then joined back together. Long, 4K, or high-frame-rate videos take noticeably longer than other tools. Keep the tab open until the export finishes.

Local processing

Video and audio are decoded, reversed, and encoded on your device. Temporary sections are kept in browser storage while the export runs and are removed afterward.

FAQ

  • Can I reverse only part of a video? No. The whole video is reversed. Trim it first if you only need part of it.
  • Which format is the output? The same container as the source when it is supported, otherwise MP4.
  • Why is the audio option unavailable? The video has no audio track, so the export is silent.
  • Why does the preview play forward? Browsers cannot play video backward smoothly, so the reversal happens during export.
