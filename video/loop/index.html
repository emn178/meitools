Loop Video

Overview

Loop Video repeats a complete video back to back, from 2 to 50 times, in a single file. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How to loop a video

Import a video, choose how many times it should play, preview it, and export it. The preview plays the video as many times as the export will.

Quality and format

When the video can be joined to itself safely, every round keeps the original picture without re-encoding, in the same format as the source. Otherwise one round is converted to MP4 once and then repeated. The audio is rebuilt as one continuous track so it stays in sync at every repeat.

Local processing

The video is processed on your device. Temporary files are kept in browser storage while the export runs and are removed afterward. The output grows with every repeat, so large videos looped many times need plenty of free space.

FAQ

  • Can I loop only part of a video? No. The whole video is repeated. Trim it first if you only need part of it.
  • Is there a gap between repeats? No. Each repeat starts right where the previous one ends.
  • Why is the output MP4? The source could not be joined to itself without converting it, so one round was converted to MP4.
