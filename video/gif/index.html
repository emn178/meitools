Convert Video to GIF

Overview

Convert Video to GIF creates a silent animated GIF from one selected part of a video. Choose its dimensions, frame rate, color quality, and whether it loops forever or plays once.

How to make a GIF from video

Import a video, choose the output dimensions and GIF options, select a range on the timeline, preview the source range, then export and save the GIF locally. Width and height stay linked to the source aspect ratio.

GIF size and quality

Choose a calculated size preset or enter a custom width or height. Smaller uses fewer colors for a potentially smaller file, Balanced offers a practical default, and Best uses the largest color palette. Longer clips, larger dimensions, higher frame rates, and more colors can create much larger files.

How GIF export works

MeiTools first samples the selected clip to build a color palette, then creates the GIF at your chosen dimensions and frame rate. The progress indicator shows these as two stages.

Local processing

The video is decoded and the GIF is encoded on your device. Your file is never uploaded, and this one-shot session is not restored after you leave or reload.

FAQ

  • Does a GIF include sound? No. GIF animation has no audio.
  • Can I enter any output size? Enter a custom width or height; the other dimension updates automatically to preserve the video aspect ratio.
  • Can the GIF loop? Choose Forever or Once from the Loop menu before exporting.
  • Why can GIF files be large? Duration, dimensions, frame rate, and color quality all affect the result.
  • Can I edit an existing GIF? No. This tool accepts one video source.
