Crop Video

Overview

Crop Video removes the outer parts of a video and keeps the selected region for the full clip. Draw directly on the preview or enter exact coordinates and dimensions.

How to crop a video

Follow these steps in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

  • Import a video file from your device.
  • Move or resize the crop region on the preview, or enter exact X, Y, width, and height values.
  • Export at the selected crop size. MeiTools uses high constant quality and preserves audio automatically.
  • Export the cropped video in the source container when supported and save it locally.

Keep source format when possible

Cropping changes every video frame, so the video must be re-encoded. MeiTools keeps the source container and video codec when its browser-based encoder supports them, and otherwise uses a compatible codec in the same container or falls back to MP4. Audio is copied when compatible and re-encoded only when required. Output dimensions and crop coordinates are adjusted to valid even values when needed.

Local processing

All decoding, cropping, and encoding runs on your device. Files are not sent to a cloud service, and the crop session is not restored after you leave or reload the page. Large or long videos may use significant memory.

FAQ

  • Which formats are supported? Common MP4, WebM, and MOV files usually work. Unsupported codecs show an error at import.
  • Does cropping reduce quality? The video must be re-encoded because its pixels change. MeiTools uses a high constant-quality setting to minimize visible loss.
  • Can I enter an exact crop size? Yes. Use the X, Y, Width, and Height fields below the preview.
