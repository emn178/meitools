Remove Video Section

Overview

Remove Video Section deletes one continuous part of a video and keeps everything before and after it in the original order. Choose the unwanted range on the timeline, preview the remaining video, and export the result.

How to remove a section from a video

Follow these steps in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

  • Import one video file from your device.
  • Drag the timeline handles or use frame controls to mark one continuous section for removal.
  • Leave some video outside the removal range, then preview the result. Preview automatically skips the unwanted section.
  • Export the remaining video and save it locally.

Keep source format when possible

MeiTools tries to preserve the source container, video codec, and audio track without quality, resolution, or format controls. Compatible parts may be copied directly. When precise encoding is required, MeiTools re-encodes the retained intervals so frames from the removed section cannot appear in the result.

Local processing

All decoding, removal, encoding, and joining runs on your device. Files are not sent to a cloud service, and the removal session is not restored after you leave or reload the page. Large, long, or high-resolution videos may use significant memory, so a desktop browser is recommended for demanding exports.

FAQ

  • How many sections can I remove? You can remove one continuous section per export.
  • Can I remove the beginning or end? Yes. Keep at least 1 ms of video outside the removal range before exporting.
  • Does removing a section reduce quality? Compatible content may be copied without quality loss. Some sources require precise re-encoding to prevent removed frames from leaking into the output.
  • Will audio be preserved? Yes. MeiTools keeps or re-encodes audio as needed so it stays synchronized with the remaining video.
  • Which formats are supported? Common MP4, WebM, and MOV files usually work. Unsupported codecs show an error at import or export.
