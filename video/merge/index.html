Merge Video

Overview

Merge Video combines multiple clips into one MP4. Arrange the clips in the order you want, choose the output canvas size, and preview the selected clip before exporting.

How to merge videos

Follow these steps in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

  • Import two or more video files from your device.
  • Drag the clips to arrange them in the order you want them to play.
  • Choose the output canvas size, then check the preview.
  • Export the merged video and save it locally.

Canvas and source formats

Clips are normalized to a shared canvas before they are joined. Videos with a different aspect ratio are letterboxed so the full picture remains visible. The export uses MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio when available, and output dimensions are adjusted to valid even values when needed.

Local processing

All decoding, normalization, and encoding runs on your device. Files are not sent to a cloud service. Merging multiple or high-resolution videos may use significant memory, so a desktop browser is recommended for demanding exports.

FAQ

  • How many videos can I merge? Import at least two clips. The practical limit depends on your device memory and the total duration and resolution.
  • Can I change the clip order? Yes. Drag clips in the list to reorder them before exporting.
  • Will different aspect ratios work? Yes. Clips are fitted to the shared canvas with letterboxing rather than being cropped.
  • Which formats are supported? Common MP4, WebM, and MOV files usually work. Unsupported codecs show an error at import or export.
