Extract Audio

Overview

Extract Audio saves the first audio track from a video entirely in your browser. Auto preserves the original audio without re-encoding when possible, or you can choose MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, or Opus.

How to extract audio

Import one video, wait for its audio track to be analyzed, choose Auto or an audio format, then export the audio file to your device.

Auto and output formats

Auto copies AAC, MP3, Opus, Vorbis, FLAC, and supported PCM audio into a conventional standalone audio file. Unknown or incompatible audio falls back to high-quality AAC in M4A. Choosing a format converts only when the source codec cannot be copied safely. Bitrate, sample rate, and channels remain automatic.

Local processing

Audio analysis, extraction, and conversion happen on your device. The video and extracted audio are never uploaded, and the session is not restored after you leave or reload the page.

FAQ

  • Does Auto reduce quality? Not when the source audio can be copied safely; in that case no audio samples are re-encoded.
  • What happens if the video has no audio? Export remains disabled and the editor reports that no audio track was found.
  • Can I choose a different audio track? No. This tool extracts the first usable audio track.
  • Can I trim or adjust volume here? No. Use Trim or Adjust Video Volume before extracting when needed.
