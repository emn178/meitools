Mute Video

Overview

Mute Video removes the audio track from a video entirely in your browser. The picture is copied without re-encoding, so video quality, resolution, and format stay exactly the same, and nothing is uploaded.

How to mute a video

Import a video, preview it without sound, and export it. There are no settings to choose.

Removing audio vs. 0% volume

Mute Video deletes the audio track, so the exported file has no sound at all. It is quick even for long videos and the file is slightly smaller. Setting volume to 0% instead keeps a silent audio track, which is useful when another editor or upload site expects every video to have one.

Local processing

The video stream is copied into a new file on your device. The original audio is not kept in the exported file.

FAQ

  • Does muting reduce video quality? No. The video stream is copied without re-encoding.
  • Which format is the output? The same container as the source when it is supported, otherwise MP4.
  • Can I mute only part of a video? No. The whole audio track is removed.
  • What happens if the video has no audio? Export is disabled because the video is already silent.
