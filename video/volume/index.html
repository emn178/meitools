Adjust Video Volume

Overview

Adjust Video Volume lets you lower, boost, or mute a complete video from 0% to 400% while keeping processing on your device. Set the volume to 0% to create a silent video, use 100% for the original level, or raise it above 100% to amplify quiet audio.

How to adjust or mute a video

Import a video, choose a preset or enter an exact percentage, preview the result, and export it. Select 0% when you want to mute the video without removing its picture.

Volume levels

The editor supports precise 1% steps. Values below 100% make the audio quieter, 0% mutes it completely, and values above 100% make it louder. Preview the result before exporting because high amplification can make already-loud audio sound distorted.

Local processing

Audio is filtered and encoded locally in your browser. Video frames are copied unchanged and files are never uploaded.

FAQ

  • How do I mute a video? Set the volume to 0%, then export the video.
  • What range is supported? 0% to 400% in 1% steps.
  • Can I change only part of a video? No. The setting applies to the complete video.
  • What happens without audio? Volume controls and export are disabled.
