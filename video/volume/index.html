Adjust Video Volume

Overview

Adjust Video Volume makes a complete video louder or quieter from 0% to 400% while keeping processing on your device. Use values below 100% to turn loud audio down, 100% for the original level, or values above 100% to boost quiet speech and recordings.

How to adjust video volume

Import a video, choose a preset or enter an exact percentage, preview the result, and export it.

Volume levels

The editor supports precise 1% steps. Values below 100% make the audio quieter and values above 100% make it louder. Preview the result before exporting because high amplification can make already-loud audio sound distorted. At 0% the video keeps a silent audio track, which some editors and upload sites expect.

Local processing

Audio is filtered and encoded locally in your browser. Video frames are copied unchanged and files are never uploaded.

FAQ

  • What range is supported? 0% to 400% in 1% steps.
  • How do I remove the sound completely? Use Mute Video to delete the audio track instead. It is faster because no audio is encoded, and the file gets smaller.
  • Can I change only part of a video? No. The setting applies to the complete video.
  • What happens without audio? Volume controls and export are disabled.
