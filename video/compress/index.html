Compress Video

Overview

Compress Video makes a video file smaller entirely in your browser. Choose how strongly to compress and, optionally, a lower resolution, then save a widely compatible MP4. Your video is never uploaded.

How to compress a video

Follow these steps on your device.

  • Import one video file.
  • Choose Smallest file, Balanced, or Better quality.
  • Optionally pick a lower resolution such as 720p or 480p for a much smaller file.
  • Export and save the compressed MP4. The result shows how much smaller it is.

Compression levels and resolution

Balanced is a practical default for sharing. Smallest file trades more detail for size, and Better quality keeps more detail with less reduction. Lowering the resolution usually saves the most space. Resolution choices only go down, never above the source, and keep the original aspect ratio.

Local processing

Decoding and encoding run on your device, so the video is not uploaded. Compression re-encodes every frame, so long or high-resolution videos can take a while and need significant memory. The session is not restored after you leave or reload the page.

FAQ

  • What format is the output? MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, which plays almost everywhere.
  • Can the file get larger? Rarely. When the source is already highly compressed, the output is capped near the source bitrate, but it may end up about the same size.
  • Can I set an exact target size? No. Choose a level and resolution instead.
  • Is audio kept? Yes. Audio is kept and compressed; silent videos stay silent.
