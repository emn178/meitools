Flip Video

Overview

Flip Video mirrors the entire picture horizontally, vertically, or in both directions. A horizontal flip swaps left and right, while a vertical flip turns the picture upside down without changing its dimensions.

How to flip a video

Follow these steps in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

  • Import one video file from your device.
  • Turn on Flip horizontally, Flip vertically, or both.
  • Play or seek through the preview to check the mirrored picture.
  • Export the flipped video and save it locally.

Output size and quality

Flipping keeps the same output width and height. Because every frame changes, the video must be re-encoded. MeiTools uses a high-quality setting, keeps the source container and video codec when compatible, and preserves audio automatically. The flip is applied permanently to the exported picture rather than relying on player-specific display behavior.

Local processing

All decoding, flipping, and encoding runs on your device. Your video is not uploaded, and the editing session is not restored after you leave or reload the page. Large, long, or high-resolution files may use significant memory, so a desktop browser is recommended for demanding exports.

FAQ

  • What does horizontal flip do? It creates a mirror image by swapping the left and right sides, which is useful for correcting mirrored camera recordings.
  • What does vertical flip do? It swaps the top and bottom of the picture. You can combine it with horizontal flip when both axes need correction.
  • Does flipping reduce quality? Flipping requires re-encoding, which can introduce a small quality change. MeiTools uses a high-quality setting to minimize visible loss.
  • Will audio be preserved? Yes. Audio is copied when compatible and re-encoded only when the output container requires it.
  • Which formats are supported? Common MP4, WebM, and MOV files usually work. If the source format cannot be reused, MeiTools chooses a compatible output automatically.
