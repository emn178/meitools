Rotate Video

Overview

Rotate Video turns the entire picture left or right in 90-degree steps. Use it to correct a sideways or upside-down recording and preview the final orientation before exporting.

How to rotate a video

Follow these steps in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

  • Import one video file from your device.
  • Use Rotate left or Rotate right until the picture has the correct orientation.
  • Play or seek through the preview to check the whole video.
  • Export the rotated video and save it locally.

Output size and quality

A 90° or 270° turn swaps the output width and height; a 180° turn keeps them unchanged. Rotating changes every frame, so the video must be re-encoded. MeiTools uses a high-quality setting, keeps the source container and video codec when compatible, and preserves audio automatically. The visible orientation is applied permanently so the exported video displays consistently in common players.

Local processing

All decoding, rotation, and encoding runs on your device. Your video is not uploaded, and the editing session is not restored after you leave or reload the page. Large, long, or high-resolution files may use significant memory, so a desktop browser is recommended for demanding exports.

FAQ

  • Which angles are supported? You can rotate by 90°, 180°, or 270°. Turn left or right more than once to reach the angle you need.
  • Does rotating reduce quality? Rotation requires re-encoding, which can introduce a small quality change. MeiTools uses a high-quality setting to minimize visible loss.
  • Will audio be preserved? Yes. Audio is copied when compatible and re-encoded only when the output container requires it.
  • Which formats are supported? Common MP4, WebM, and MOV files usually work. If the source format cannot be reused, MeiTools chooses a compatible output automatically.
  • Can I rotate a video on a phone? Yes, although long or high-resolution videos are safer to process on a desktop device.
